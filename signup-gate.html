Community Code of Conduct
Version 1.0 — Last updated May 19, 2026

For All Users

Respect: Treat every person with dignity and respect regardless of age, race, gender, religion, ability, or background.

Honesty: Provide truthful information in your profile. Do not misrepresent your identity, credentials, or intentions. Do not fabricate reviews.

Safety: Report any unsafe conduct, abuse, or violations to MatConnect immediately. Do not meet in unsafe or isolated locations.

For Coaches

  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries at all times
  • Never conduct one-on-one sessions with a minor in a private setting without a parent/guardian present or explicit authorization
  • Complete SafeSport or equivalent youth protection training
  • Arrive on time and come prepared to every session
  • Do not use abusive or inappropriate language with athletes

For Parents and Guardians

  • Be present during training sessions, especially for younger athletes
  • Communicate expectations and athlete limitations to the Coach
  • Do not harass or pressure Coaches through the Platform

Violations

Violations may result in a formal warning, account suspension, permanent ban, or reporting to law enforcement. Reports involving potential child abuse will be referred to the appropriate authorities.
